Privacy Policy
===

Thank you for using Symbol Scribe. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information while you use our app. This privacy policy explains how Symbol Scribe handles data and what we do not collect.

## Information We Collect

Symbol Scribe is designed as a local macOS utility for browsing and copying symbols, emoji, and kaomoji. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, account details, or usage profile.

### a. **Local Data Storage**

- Symbol Scribe may store small local preferences and user-selected items such as favorites or recent history on your device.
- This data is stored locally on your Mac and is used only to improve convenience within the app.
- We do not upload this data to remote servers or third-party services.

### b. **Clipboard Usage**

When you copy a symbol or emoji, it is placed into the macOS clipboard. This is a system-level operation handled by macOS and is not transmitted to us or to any external server.

### c. **Log Data**

We do not collect anonymous usage logs, crash reports, analytics data, or other telemetry from the app.

## Use of Data

Symbol Scribe does not sell, rent, or share personal information. Any local data stored in the app is used only for app functionality such as favorites, history, or settings on your device.

## Third-Party Services

Symbol Scribe does not integrate with advertising networks, analytics providers, or external data collection services. Your data is not shared with third parties.

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data stored locally on your device. Since Symbol Scribe does not send user data to external servers, the primary protection comes from your device's operating system and local storage security.

## User Control

- You can delete favorites, recent history, or app settings at any time from the app or your device.
- No data is backed up to the cloud by this app.
- If you uninstall Symbol Scribe, local app data stored on your device can be removed with the app uninstall process.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update this page so you can review the latest version. 

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or the app, please contact us at: [kennyiseeyou@gmail.com](mailto:kennyiseeyou@gmail.com).

This policy is effective from January 4, 2025.
